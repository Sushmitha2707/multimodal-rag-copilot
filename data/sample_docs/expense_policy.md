# Acme Corp Travel and Expense Policy

## Daily allowances
The meal allowance is Rs 1,200 per day for domestic travel and Rs 3,000 per day for international travel.

## Accommodation
The hotel cap is Rs 7,500 per night in metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi and Chennai) and Rs 5,000 per
night in all other cities.

## Air travel
Employees fly economy class on flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights longer than
6 hours, but only with approval from a vice president.

## Filing claims
Expense claims must be filed within 30 days of the expense. Original receipts are required for every item
above Rs 500.

## Reimbursement
Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days, paid into the employee's salary account.

## Client entertainment
Client entertainment is limited to Rs 2,500 per person and needs prior approval from the department head.
