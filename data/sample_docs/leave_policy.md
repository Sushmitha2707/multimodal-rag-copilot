# Acme Corp Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing at 2 days per month.
Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year. Carried-forward days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when the absence lasts
more than 2 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days and must be taken within
6 months of the child's birth.

## Applying for leave
Leave of more than 5 working days must be requested at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal and
approved by the line manager. Shorter leave needs only manager approval before the leave starts.

## Public holidays
Acme Corp observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published every December.
