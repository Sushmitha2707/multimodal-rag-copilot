# Acme Corp IT Security Standard

## Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are rotated every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
is mandatory for VPN access and for company email.

## Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT Security desk within 2 hours. IT will start a
remote wipe of the device as soon as the report is received.

## Removable media
USB storage devices are blocked by default. Exceptions require written approval from the CISO.

## Phishing
Suspicious emails should be reported with the "Report Phish" button in the mail client. The security team
responds within 4 business hours.

## Personal devices (BYOD)
Personal devices may access company data only after enrolling in the MDM system. Full-disk encryption must be
enabled on every enrolled device.

## Training
All employees complete security awareness training within 30 days of joining and then once every year.
