# Acme Corp New Joiner Handbook

## First day
On day one, IT issues your laptop and Facilities issues your access badge. The orientation session starts at
10:00 AM in the main training room.

## Probation
The probation period is 6 months, with formal performance reviews at the 3-month and 6-month marks.

## Buddy programme
Every new joiner is paired with a buddy for their first 90 days to help with questions about tools and culture.

## Mandatory training
The Code of Conduct course must be completed within 7 days of joining. Information Security and POSH awareness
courses must be completed within 30 days.

## Working hours and location
Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00. The hybrid policy requires 3 office days per week: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

## Benefits
Group health insurance starts on the date of joining and covers the employee, spouse, two children and parents,
with a sum insured of Rs 5 lakh. Salary is credited on the last working day of each month.
